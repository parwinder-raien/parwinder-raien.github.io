Inside Performance Marketing

Who We Are

CliqBriz is being built by people who understand performance marketing from the inside. Our experience comes from living within the affiliate and performance marketing ecosystem — learning from the nuances of tracking infrastructure, addressing conversion discrepancies, and continuously building cleaner ways to evaluate campaign health.

We don't believe in generic tools layered on top of outdated legacy architectures. We focus on building practical, deterministic software that helps advertisers, networks, agencies, and publishers execute campaigns with total confidence.

Engineered for Accuracy

Reliable attribution built for high-scale operations.

What We Believe

Every click represents intent, budget, and potential revenue. When tracking tools become slow, unreliable, or opaque, everyone in the value chain loses. Precision is non-negotiable — every single click deserves accurate attribution and real-time clarity.

How We Think

We look at performance tracking as core infrastructure: it must be fast, quiet, transparent, and resilient enough to handle high-throughput traffic spikes across the globe without latency, data drops, or unexpected downtime.
