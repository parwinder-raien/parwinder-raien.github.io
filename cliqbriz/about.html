Who We Are

CliqBriz is being built by people who understand performance marketing from the inside. Our experience comes from living within the affiliate and performance marketing ecosystem — learning from the nuances of tracking infrastructure, addressing conversion discrepancies, and continuously building cleaner ways to evaluate campaign health.

We focus on building practical, deterministic software that helps advertisers, networks, agencies, and publishers execute campaigns with total confidence.

What We Believe

Every click represents intent, budget, and potential revenue. When tracking tools become slow, unreliable, or opaque, everyone in the value chain loses. Precision is non-negotiable.

How We Think

We look at performance infrastructure as a utility: it should be fast, quiet, transparent, and resilient enough to handle high-throughput traffic without unexpected downtime.

Our Vision: Know the Value of Every Click

Our vision is simple: To track a single click and understand the value behind it. We empower performance marketers to move fluidly through the entire campaign cycle:
